## What's new

- **Profile on the side.** Click an agent's name to open its profile next to the chat, with a big avatar and four tabs: Activity, Memory, Background and Settings.
- **Memory at a glance.** A small graph of the notes each agent has made or saved.
- **See a background before you set it.** Pick a colour or photo and the chat previews it live; Cancel or Set.
- **You, at the bottom.** Your account is one long pill at the foot of the sidebar.
