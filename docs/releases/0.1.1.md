## What's new

- **Setup flows.** The bubble moves from page to page. Simple and Advanced are shown side by side, every AI is shown with its logo, and Connect really connects.
- **A shorter tour.** Four hands-on steps. Skip ends it for good.
- **Calmer look.** More room around the edges, plainer buttons, one voice button in the chat bar, Help in the account menu.
- **Make agents your way.** New agent is one sheet with the full look editor, the AI, templates and what it watches.
- **See the agent's computer.** The computer button in a chat opens its screen beside the chat.
- **Clear failures.** When a run fails, the chat says why and offers one fix. Computers retry instead of giving up.
- **Agents start on your device.** The shared computer is only made when an agent needs a desktop.
- **Phones.** Many iPhone and Android fixes: downloads, photos, re-pairing, the keyboard on New agent, and an offline screen.
