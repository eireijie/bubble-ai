## What's new

- **A roomier sidebar.** Your agents sit in a grid of big bubbles at the top, with chats in folders below. Chats, Computers, Memory, Marketplace and More are icons at the bottom.
- **A cleaner narrow sidebar.** Drag it all the way in and it keeps just a + and your initials, with even space around the window buttons. Your initials open everything else.
- **Glass buttons.** + and your initials are clear glass that bends the background behind them.
- **A calmer chat.** Your agent's face sits above its name at the top. Messages and the chat bar share one centred column, and a ↓ button jumps to the newest message.
- **Right-click a chat** to pin it, mark it unread, hide it, or move it to a folder without leaving the menu.
- **Agent photos.** Give an agent a photo instead of a bubble, and it shows everywhere.
- **New agent in one sheet**, a short agent panel, and a simpler Bubble look page.
- **Take control of a computer** while your agent is using it, then give it back.
- **Settings in one sheet**, with Import memory to bring over what another assistant knows about you.
- **Hermes in one tap.** Don't have Hermes? Tap Install and Bubble AI sets it up on your computer, already using the model you connected.
