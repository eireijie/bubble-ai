## What's new

- **Fewer buttons up top.** The sidebar is a slim icon strip (⌘1–5). "Needs you" only shows when something does.
- **Pop-ups instead of pages.** Settings, agents and most other pages open as pop-ups sized to what they hold.
- **Smaller, tighter text** outside the home page.
- **One chat box.** Attach, typing, dictation and voice calls sit in one box.
- **Roomier New agent.** The look is set in simple rows, with extras under More options.
- **Tour fix.** Pressing / or @ keeps the popup up long enough to see it.
- **Opens where you left off.** The app opens your last chat.
- **Phones.** A new welcome screen with floating bubbles: scan a QR code or type a link.
