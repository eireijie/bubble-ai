## What's new

- **Connect apps.** One window, opened from the bottom of the sidebar, for every app and skill. Search, filter, and connect in one tap. Composio comes first for more apps and easy sign-in.
- **One background.** The chat's colour, photo or plain background now fills the whole window, and the sidebar is a see-through dark layer on top of it.
- **Calmer menus.** Smaller, tighter menus that open instantly and match the sidebar.
- **Soft colour backgrounds.** Colours are now a gentle gradient instead of a flat fill.
- **The sidebar shows the last message** under each agent's name, and search is a small icon.
- **Bigger pinned agents**, with the unread dot sitting neatly inside the highlight.
- **A more personal "Make it yours".** Pick what your agent should help with, tell it a bit about you, and choose how it talks and when it checks in. It then suggests which apps to connect.
